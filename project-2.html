U.S. Rising Temperatures Thematic Map Details:

Fall 2023

Problem

The temperatures of the United States has risen over the last two decades and this project was to discover the trends to compare the rate of change in temperatures to temperature increase.

My Role

Created a thematic map project to display multiple layers featuring the temperature increase as well as the rate of change to show the gradual changes in temperature for the United States and displayed the information found through ArcGIS StoryMaps of the information found about the temperature increase throughout the semester.

Data & Method

Researched the rising temperatures over the years in the United States and compared the temperature rise to its rate of change.

The research and the map were placed inside of a StoryMap to present the findings both from maps and other sources. Here's the StoryMap:

Map

What I Found

I found that the United States warmed up 68% faster than any other country in the past 50 years. Around 1991, wildfires became a yearly trend almost appearing every year from then to 2022.

What I Would Do Differently

Since this was my first project using ArcGIS Online, I would like to make the map cleaner in the format rather than using symbols to represent another layer over the top of another. I would also like to improve the StoryMap as it appears lacking with detail than it should for the research.
