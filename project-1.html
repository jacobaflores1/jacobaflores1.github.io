Texas Housing Crisis

Spring 2026

Problem

Housing in Texas has been in demand in the recent years and our research was to find the cheapest areas as well as the homes with the most value.

My Role

Designed maps of Texas County Housing Values for 2014, 2024, & 2034 using HTML & CSS for map design & interactive map features to switch years and calculated predicted exponential values for 2034 through Google Sheets using the compound annual growth rate formula.

Data & Method

Predicted 2034 data for Texas Housing Prices and Values using historical data from 2014 and 2024 to provide information for future homeowners about the best counties for living in the state of Texas.

My part was to find the values of the homes from each county of Texas as well as population numbers. Here's the value map below:

Map

What I Found

In the central part of Texas, the greatest value increase is shown on the map from the predicted 2034 data. Also, the value of homes appear less in the west than in the east, possibly because the areas are away from the coast.

What I Would Do Differently

I would have wanted to add extra components like UI to make the map look cleaner in detail and combining the other map in my team where they calculated the Price Asked data.
