Texas A&M FEMA Flood Zones Details:

Spring 2026

Problem

Flood Zones at Texas A&M University may not appear at the exact location which it is located and is not effective compared to manual results.

My Role

Setting each layer for the location, I had to record around the flood zone located near the golf course at Texas A&M of three different results of the boundaries.

Data & Method

First, the FEMA flood layer was set up using ArcGIS Field Maps. Then, manually recorded three layers around the location to closely record accuracy of the flood zone.

Here's the flood zone map with each of my three layers below:

Map

What I Found

First recording for the flood zone appeared differently than the last two recorded results as this was my first trial attempt. Additionally, the location appeared still wet over the land which may be affected by the flood zone nearby.

What I Would Do Differently

I would like to have known how to record around the boundaries for the first trial so I would have not recorded too much towards the land cover of the grass parts of the boundary.
